BIKEPARK MANAGER - how to play
==============================

Nothing to install. Unzip this folder anywhere and start the game:

WINDOWS
  Double-click BikeparkManager.exe (keep the "data" folder next to it).
  If "Windows protected your PC" appears: click "More info", then "Run anyway".

MAC
  Drag "Bikepark Manager.app" into Applications (or anywhere) and double-click it.
  If macOS says it "can't be opened" or "can't check it for malicious software":
  open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway". Only needed once.

CONTROLS
  Move: WASD / arrow keys or middle-drag    Zoom: mouse wheel    Rotate: Q/E or right-drag
  Pause: Space    Speed: 1-4    Menus: the bottom bar    Close: Esc
  Click riders, trails, lifts or buildings to open their window.

Your career is saved automatically when you quit.
